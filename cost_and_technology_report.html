4 Achieving "Zero-Lag" Live Transcription & Subtitles
How Traditional Apps Lag (1.5 - 3.0 sec)
Standard apps wait for the speaker to stop talking, record the entire WAV audio file, upload it to a server, wait for translation, and then show a caption. This causes an agonizing 2 to 3-second delay.
How AmorBridge Eliminates Lag (150 - 300 ms)
We use Continuous Interim Streaming (EXTRA_PARTIAL_RESULTS). Words are transcribed phoneme-by-phoneme as they leave the speaker's mouth. Translated subtitles appear almost instantaneously!
5 Incoming Call Notification Architecture (Instagram / WhatsApp Style)

To make incoming calls wake up the phone screen just like Instagram, WhatsApp, or standard cellular calls, the app uses Android's highest-tier telephony mechanisms:

  • Android CallStyle Notification: Bypasses "Do Not Disturb" (when permitted) and rings the phone with custom ringtone and vibration.
  • Full-Screen Intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): If the phone is locked or asleep in her bag, Android automatically lights up the display and presents the full-screen call ringing UI with caller avatar and Accept/Decline action buttons.
  • Telecom / Foreground Service: Keeps the audio/video call alive without Android OS power management killing the background process.
